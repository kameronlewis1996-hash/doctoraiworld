The story

A personal start.
An independent build.

I started this project about 18 months ago to help my wife. About six months ago, I began working seriously on turning that idea into DoctorAI World.

I’ve been building it independently, with a straightforward aim: make medications, health information and appointment preparation easier to manage in one place.

What began as something for my wife has grown into a project I want to make useful for other people and families too. DoctorAI World is still developing, and it’s designed to support conversations with healthcare professionals, not replace them.
