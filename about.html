A note from the founder

Why we’re doing this.

A simple place to organise the details you want to bring to a health conversation.

KL

Kam (Kameron Lewis)DoctorAI founder · Civil construction · Taupō, New Zealand

Where it began

The little things between visits add up.

I’m Kam. I work in civil construction in Taupō, and I’ve spent the last 18 months building DoctorAI around work and family life.

My family’s experience managing health needs made me think about what happens between appointments. Keeping a medicine list, remembering a symptom, or writing down a question can take effort—especially when work and family already fill the day.

I wanted to explore a more practical way to gather the details a person chooses to keep, so they can find them when they are getting ready to talk with a health professional.

Who it’s for

Everyday people, and the professionals who care for them.

Health questions can come up at any age. Long workdays and family commitments can make it harder to set aside time for an appointment. DoctorAI is being built to help people organise information they choose to keep and prepare questions when the time is right. The aim is to support busy doctors and other health professionals by helping bring a person’s own notes into the conversation.

↗

The role of DoctorAI

A bridge to a conversation, not a replacement for care.

DoctorAI helps organise information you choose to enter and prepare for a conversation with a professional. It is not a doctor, does not diagnose or prescribe, and does not replace professional advice. Doctors and health professionals bring their training and the wider context to decisions about care.
