Your information and choices

Delete your data and request account deletion

You can delete your Health Hub records and private documents from Privacy controls. Deleting hub data, requesting account deletion and cancelling a subscription are separate actions.

Delete Health Hub records and private files

  1. Open Profile in the Health Hub. If you have an account copy or uploaded documents, sign in with the Google account you used for DoctorAI.
  2. Open Privacy controls. Export your hub data and download any files you want to keep before deletion. The hub export contains records and file details; it does not include the uploaded file contents.
  3. Choose Delete my hub data and confirm. Keep the page open and stay connected while private files and your account copy are removed.
  4. Wait for the result. If deletion is incomplete, retry while signed in and connected. Some files may already have been removed; the remaining local record is kept so you can retry.

Deleting while signed out clears only this session or device. It does not remove an account copy. Other devices can also hold local copies: clear those separately before using them again.

Request account deletion

Email support@doctoraiworld.com with the subject “DoctorAI account deletion request”. Use the email address associated with your DoctorAI Google sign-in so support can verify the account. Do not include medical records, passwords or payment-card details in the request.

The in-app action removes hub information and documents; it does not itself confirm closure of your sign-in account or deletion of billing records. Ask support to confirm what has been removed and explain any records that must be retained.

Cancel a subscription separately

Use Manage or cancel subscription on the Pro page. Deleting health information does not cancel a Stripe subscription. Existing uploaded files remain available to view, download and delete after Pro expires; new uploads require Pro.

Data held by other providers

Deleting DoctorAI records cannot recall copied text, downloaded PDFs or files you have shared. It also does not directly delete provider logs or records held by Google, Stripe or OpenAI. The privacy notice explains these services and the limits of deletion, including OpenAI’s default API abuse-monitoring retention.
