Organisation, not diagnosis

DoctorAI helps you organise health information and understand general health education. It is an AI service, not a doctor, emergency service, diagnosis, or treatment provider.

Professional care

Do not start, stop, or change prescription medication based only on DoctorAI. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical decisions.

Your information

You control what you save, edit, delete, or share. Health Memory is optional. When enabled, approved details may be included with your AI question. Review information for accuracy before relying on it.

Urgent situations

If you may be experiencing an emergency or severe symptoms, contact local emergency services immediately. Do not wait for an AI response.

AI limitations

AI responses can be incomplete or wrong. Symptom groupings reflect notes you entered and do not establish that one event caused another. Use the service for general education and preparation for a conversation with a clinician, not as a substitute for professional advice.
