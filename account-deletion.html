Account and data requests

Request account deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your DoctorAI World account and associated account data. This is different from deleting health data stored only on your device.

Send a deletion request

Email us from the address linked to your Google sign-in. Include that sign-in email only. Do not send health details, medical documents, or payment-card information.

Email DoctorAI support to request deletion ↗

The button opens a draft in your email app. Your request is sent only if you choose Send. You can also email support@doctoraiworld.com.

What the request covers

Account deletion applies to the DoctorAI account identity and associated health records, private documents, and entitlement records in systems DoctorAI controls. It does not delete the Google account you use to sign in.

Some billing, security, or payment records may need to be retained under applicable law or provider requirements. Any retained category and its reason should be explained in the response. Stripe payment records and provider-held logs require separate handling where supported.

Subscriptions and copies on your devices

A deletion request does not itself cancel an active Pro subscription or request a refund. Manage or cancel it from the subscription page, or mention it in your email so support can address renewal before account closure.

DoctorAI cannot remotely remove information stored only on an offline device or in a file you exported. While signed in and online, use the app’s Delete my health data control on each device, or clear the app’s local data afterward.

Account restoration and privacy

DoctorAI retains a pseudonymous deletion marker to prevent old sessions or billing events from restoring a closed account. The same sign-in email cannot open another account after closure. Read the Privacy Notice for more information about data handling and retention.

This page is for DoctorAI World account-deletion requests. Deleting health data does not delete your account; deleting your DoctorAI account does not delete your Google account.
