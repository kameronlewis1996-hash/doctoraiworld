## Demo path for the preview

1. Open Health Hub and use **Visit prep → Build my visit brief**. Add a fictional concern and select only fictional records intended for the brief. Review before copying or printing. Status and certainty are self-reported.
2. Use **Visit prep → Prepare an appointment request**. Enter an invented reason, review, then copy. The clinic must be contacted separately; no appointment is booked or transmitted.
3. Open `/professional-brief-demo.html` for a static example of a professional-facing layout. Every detail is invented; the page has no patient connection.
4. In **My Health → Health profile**, review how current, historical and unsure conditions and allergy reactions are labelled. The terminology subset is intentionally small and non-diagnostic.

## Manual acceptance still needed

1. With at least two invented conditions and allergies in account A, open a visit brief and select exactly one condition and one allergy. Confirm the selected count and that the other condition/allergy are absent from review. Go back with keyboard focus returned to the agenda, then cancel. Reopen to confirm no old selection remains. Switch to account B and confirm account A's brief and temporary print copy are absent.
2. Prepare an invented appointment request, review it, go back, cancel, then reopen to confirm a blank draft. Test allowed and denied clipboard access. Confirm no appointment was added and no clinic request was sent.
3. Repeat at a 390px viewport with keyboard and screen reader, checking labels, focus order, 44px controls, review text, print/PDF output and service-worker refresh. Open the brief from Today or Diary, close it, and confirm focus lands on the visible Visit prep heading. The preview build and synthetic checks do not establish these browser outcomes.
4. With fictional data in two local account namespaces, choose **Use this session only** and confirm both browser copies are removed while any signed-in account copy remains. Test cancellation and a browser removal refusal. Then test **Delete my hub data** while already in session-only mode, and verify the active account copy, uploaded files, cached medicine check and temporary visit drafts are gone. Repeat with a failed document/state request; it must show incomplete deletion and retain a local retry/export copy.

## Release gate

Keep PR 75 as a draft preview until real browser/device checks, authenticated storage rehearsal, live billing verification, privacy review, terminology rights review and clinical sign-off are complete. The medication check must continue to show unknown coverage honestly. No preview result should be described as a comprehensive medicine safety assessment.
