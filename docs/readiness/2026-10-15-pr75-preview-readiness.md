## Demo path for the preview

1. Open Health Hub and use **Visit prep → Build my visit brief**. Add a fictional concern and select only fictional records intended for the brief. Review before copying or printing. Status and certainty are self-reported.
2. Use **Visit prep → Prepare an appointment request**. Enter an invented reason, review, then copy. The clinic must be contacted separately; no appointment is booked or transmitted.
3. Open `/professional-brief-demo.html` for a static example of a professional-facing layout. Every detail is invented; the page has no patient connection.
4. In **My Health → Health profile**, review how current, historical and unsure conditions and allergy reactions are labelled. The terminology subset is intentionally small and non-diagnostic.

## Release gate

Keep PR 75 as a draft preview until real browser/device checks, authenticated storage rehearsal, live billing verification, privacy review, terminology rights review and clinical sign-off are complete. The medication check must continue to show unknown coverage honestly. No preview result should be described as a comprehensive medicine safety assessment.
