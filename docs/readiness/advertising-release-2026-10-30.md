## Accessibility focus regression

The synthetic Chromium suite previously checked focus immediately after the medication dialog closed, before the app's scheduled animation-frame restoration, and could start keyboard input before signed-out session verification and route focus had settled. It now waits for the expected opener after adding, the active heading after an edited row is replaced, and signed-out readiness before keyboard interactions; it also verifies that keyboard focus actually reaches each requested control before checking its visible ring. These checks do not establish real-device or screen-reader accessibility. Verify the final release independently at 200% zoom and on physical devices.

### Measurement proposal — approval required before implementation

Success is a genuine paid subscription shown in the correct live Stripe account, paired with successful entitlement delivery. The first useful action is a separate activation hypothesis: a visitor deliberately completes a visit brief or adds a manual detail. Without consented instrumentation, neither visitor counts nor activation rates can be inferred from missing events. Existing optional resource-page counts are partial visits, not activations or purchases.

For a future privacy review, consider aggregate counts of consented landing visits, deliberate first-action completion, plan review and verified subscription activation; specify denominators and date windows, and compare aggregate counts rather than individual journeys. Attribution, if approved, should use only coarse campaign labels chosen in advance, with no advertising identifiers, cross-site identifiers, referrer URLs, free text, medicine names, health records, account IDs, email addresses or payment details. Do not send Health Hub interaction data to ad platforms. Count duplicate actions and late billing events consistently, disclose missing and blocked measurement, and never infer a medical outcome or claim causation from a small sample.

Acceptance before enabling any new measurement: documented purpose and lawful basis; independent privacy/security review; per-visit consent and GPC/DNT treatment appropriate to the design; clear opt-out; event schema and vendor destination reviewed for accidental health or identity data; short documented retention and restricted aggregate access; isolated synthetic tests for consent, refusal, retries, deduplication and absence of sensitive fields; and a user-approved deployment. This is a design proposal only: no events, analytics SDK or tracker are added by this draft.
