# Top-100 prescribing coverage audit — rights-filtered edition

The earlier audit table included Health New Zealand dispensing counts. A dataset-specific licence for commercial reuse of those figures was not confirmed in the source material available for this integration, so the ranked list, individual counts, totals, and derived coverage figures have been removed from this candidate.

This report intentionally contains no copy of that table and no derived counts. The source can be reviewed at [Health New Zealand: Pharmaceutical Collections](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/health-data-and-statistics/health-service-use/pharmaceutical-collections). Reintroduce a ranked coverage analysis only after confirming the exact dataset licence and attribution conditions for the relevant release.

The medication catalogue bundled with this candidate is the Pharmac October 2026 community and hospital schedule, separately attributed under CC BY 4.0. This catalogue source does not establish coverage of the Health New Zealand dispensing dataset. No medication-safety coverage or clinical-validation claim is made here.
