### Official terms and page-date findings checked 2026-10-17

- The [NHS website terms](https://www.nhs.uk/our-policies/terms-and-conditions/) say NHS website content is generally made available under Open Government Licence v3, subject to stated exclusions. Their attribution and refresh requirements differ for unchanged copies and adaptations, and they warn that adapting content can affect its formal clinical approval. The rights reviewer must confirm that each exact page and the app's derived wording fall within the license, check for excluded third-party material, and record the applicable attribution and refresh requirements. The NHS terms also say the site is intended for people in England; its service context cannot be assumed to fit New Zealand.
- The [NHS SPS terms](https://sps.nhs.uk/terms-and-conditions/) say SPS content is copyright protected unless otherwise stated. They permit most protected material in limited contexts such as research, private study, or internal NHS circulation, describe the information as intended for NHS healthcare professionals, and prohibit commercial or marketing use. Third-party material is excluded from that permission. The terms welcome links but prohibit suggesting SPS endorsement. Resolve whether this feature's use is permitted in DoctorAI; otherwise obtain written SPS permission or remove the SPS-derived context before release.
