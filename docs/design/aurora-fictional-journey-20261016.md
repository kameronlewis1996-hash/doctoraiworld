# Fictional Health Hub Journey - 2026-10-16

Run date: 2026-10-15 UTC. Target: fresh worktree preview at `http://127.0.0.1:4188/`. This was an anonymous, session-only run. Every entered value was explicitly synthetic; no real patient data, credentials, payment, appointment booking, sharing, print, copy, or external medication check was used.

## Journey and evidence

1. On Today (`#today`), chose **Enter details manually**. Saved medicine name `Synthetic QA sample not a real medicine` and strength/dose `QA placeholder only`; ingredients and supply were left blank. The form closed with `Medication reminder saved.` and Today reported one notice to review.
2. Opened My Health (`#health`) and expanded **Today's reminders**. It showed the saved synthetic entry, `Follow the prescription label`, and status `Due`.
3. Opened My Health / Diary (`#symptoms`) / **Add symptom**. Added `Fictional test observation - no real patient` and context `Synthetic QA entry, created only for local flow validation`; left intensity/time unrecorded. The app reported `Symptom added to your private diary.` and displayed one entry.
4. Opened My Health / Medicines (`#medications`). The list showed one active record and correctly labeled its ingredients and supply as unrecorded. The **Limited local medication check** panel described its narrow coverage and cautioned that no alert does not mean safe. **Check saved medicines** was unavailable: `Sign in with active Pro access and wait for private account data before checking.` I did not sign in, opt into sending medicine/dose/profile terms, or start a check. Opening Alerts (`#alerts`) showed one notice: `Medicine identity is not fully matched`; `No exact terminology match`; `0 of 1 medicine entries matched the local catalogue. Coverage remains limited.` Its next step was to compare the complete name, formulation, and ingredients with the original label and a pharmacist.
5. Opened Visit Prep (`#visit-prep`) / **Build my visit brief**. The form said three records were available and nothing was selected automatically. Selected only the fictional diary entry and saved medicine details, leaving the dated medicine-note record unchecked. Added a QA-only focus (`Fictional QA run: verify selected entries in the draft.`) and question (`Does the brief include only the entries I selected?`). **Review my visit brief** displayed those two selected records and both texts, with the app's self-reported/incomplete-record disclaimer. Print/save and copy controls were visible but not used; the page states nothing is sent automatically.
6. Removed both primary entries using their in-app **Delete** controls and confirmed the test-record prompts. Diary returned to `0 entries`; medicine deletion showed `Medication removed from your health hub.` and My Health showed `No medications due today`. Returning to Today showed `No saved details have been assessed yet` with no review-needed notice. This first run did not inspect automatically generated history, so it does not establish that every test record was removed.

## Functional finding and limits

The requested path completed on fresh preview 4188, including return through My Health's secondary destinations. No reproducible navigation or record-entry failure was observed. The active-Pro gate prevented testing the actual medication check in this anonymous session, and the consent copy explicitly describes server-bound fields. The combined-build rerun below found an additional generated-history cleanup limitation. This is a functional journey check, not clinical validation, medication-safety testing, a complete accessibility audit, or a visual-fidelity sign-off.

Existing local regression scripts also passed: `verify-five-tabs.cjs`, `verify-record-preparation.cjs`, `verify-health-state-contract.cjs`, and `verify-local-medication-ui.cjs`. These are automated local checks; they do not enable or validate the gated medication check.
