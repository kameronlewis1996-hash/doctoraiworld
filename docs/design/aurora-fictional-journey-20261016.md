Print/save and copy controls were visible but not used; the page states nothing is sent automatically.
6. Cleaned up both records using their in-app **Delete** controls and confirmed the test-record prompts. Diary returned to `0 entries`; medicine deletion showed `Medication removed from your health hub.` and My Health showed `No medications due today`. Returning to Today showed `No saved details have been assessed yet` with no review-needed notice. No records remain in this session.

## Functional finding and limits

The requested path completed on fresh preview 4188, including return through My Health's secondary destinations. No reproducible navigation or record-entry failure was observed. The active-Pro gate is the only journey limitation: it prevents testing the actual medication check in this anonymous session, and the consent copy explicitly describes server-bound fields. This is a functional journey check, not clinical validation, medication-safety testing, a complete accessibility audit, or a visual-fidelity sign-off.
