Supported Pro, with a sustainable plan

We are preparing lifetime complimentary Pro for disabled adults on their own accounts and eligible families. It would require manual eligibility review, with no payment method, automatic approval or marketing consent. Approval would remain valid while DoctorAI operates, without routine proof renewal.

Applications are closed while eligibility, funding, private review and retention decisions are pending. Do not send proof through email, AI chat or health-document uploads.

Eligibility confirmation

Use only a reference from the approved review channel. No diagnosis, medical history, child name, date of birth or file is requested here. If evidence is needed, its private reviewer and deletion deadline must be approved first.

Funded places and usage

New places would open only within a funded allocation. AI requests, label scans, stored documents and review time need explicit allowances and cost review. Closing future intake or reducing new allocations must not erase existing approved lifetime grants.

Actual provider usage, storage and review time can be counted separately. Cost reporting needs approved rates and invoices; no cost savings or waived subscription value are presented as cash contributions.

Approval would not automatically cancel an existing paid subscription. Any billing change would need your explicit instruction.

Future contribution goal

The planning goal is to contribute 5% of cash profit. It is not an active donation scheme or a claimed charity partnership. The financial year, approved cash-profit definition, payout timing and recipient are undecided.
