Is the Health Hub free?

The core Health Hub is free for organising notes, symptoms, medicines and appointments. Optional Pro tools include label scanning and private document uploads. Compare Free and Pro.

Does my brief get sent to a clinician?

Creating a visit brief does not send it to a clinician. Review it, then decide whether to copy, print or show it to someone.

Does DoctorAI provide medical care?

DoctorAI helps with health organisation and education. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace professional advice. A clinician should interpret health information in context.
