New Zealand doctor appointment checklist: what to bring

Use this simple checklist to get ready for a GP or other health care visit. Choose the items that are useful to you. Ticks stay in this page and are not saved.

A few details can help you feel prepared

Before you go

  • Note the date, time, health professional or clinic, and where you need to go.

  • Bring a list you already use, or write down the names you want to discuss. Our free blank medicine-list template has room for label details and questions.

  • A short reminder can help you bring up the things that matter to you.

  • You can take a support person or whānau member with you. If you would like language support, ask about a translator.

At the visit

The official guide suggests saying if you do not understand, if a drawing could help, or if you are having problems with medicines or treatment or cannot afford them. It also suggests noting what to do next.

Source and scope

This checklist uses an original layout and is based on the Health Quality & Safety Commission Te Tāhū Hauora guide, “Let’s plan for your next health care visit”. The official page provides translated and accessible versions.

This is an AI-assisted summary of that public resource, not a clinical review or medical advice. For the complete guidance, use the official resource or ask your health care team.
