For clinicians

A patient prepared starting point for your care conversation

DoctorAI helps people keep their own health details together and choose what to bring to an appointment. The free visit brief is built on the patient’s device from selected records and their own questions. Preparing it does not require an AI request.

What the patient controls

  1. Select relevant saved medicines, symptoms, measurements, allergies, conditions and notes.
  2. Add visit priorities and questions, then review and correct the brief.
  3. Choose to copy it, print it or save a PDF using their device’s print options. Nothing is sent to a clinician automatically.

How to interpret the brief

Information is patient-prepared and may be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Patient review is not clinical verification. Saved medicine details can include label text that the patient checked; scanning is text capture and does not establish product identity, medicine suitability or safety.

Use the recorded dates, sources and gaps as a starting point for reconciliation. An empty field does not establish a negative finding. No medication alert does not establish that medicines are safe together.

Access and clinical boundaries

This feature does not give clinicians an account, practice inbox or access to a patient’s DoctorAI records. The patient chooses what leaves their device. A copied or downloaded brief cannot be recalled after sharing.

DoctorAI is for organisation and education. It does not diagnose, prescribe, continuously monitor health or replace professional assessment. Clinical effectiveness and time savings have not been established.
